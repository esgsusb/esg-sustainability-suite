# Board / CSO ESG Pack

Keep board reporting decision-oriented. Recommended sections: executive signals; regulatory/standard changes; material ESG risks/opportunities; climate/GHG dashboard; target trajectory; major incidents/control exceptions; project/SPV performance; sustainable-finance implications; disclosure readiness; decisions required.

For each decision show: issue, evidence, financial/operational relevance, options, dependencies, owner, deadline and residual risk. Separate management information from external disclosure claims.
