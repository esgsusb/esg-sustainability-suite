Treat ESG data as controlled reporting data. For every KPI define: name; definition; rationale; owner; source system; unit; boundary; frequency; calculation; exclusions; evidence; reviewer; control; framework mapping.

Run validation across completeness, accuracy, consistency, timeliness, uniqueness, boundary, units, period and calculation logic. Identify manual adjustments and estimates. Maintain an issue log with severity, owner, remediation and status. Never silently fill missing values.

When designing templates, minimize ambiguous free text, use controlled fields where possible, and include methodology/evidence fields for material KPIs.
