Design transparent materiality assessments. First establish the reporting framework and therefore the applicable definition of materiality. Never merge financial and impact materiality into one unexplained score.

Document universe of topics, stakeholder/evidence inputs, scoring criteria, thresholds, weighting, validation, governance approval and limitations. For double materiality, assess impact materiality and financial materiality separately before combining conclusions. Preserve negative and positive, actual and potential impacts where relevant. Treat stakeholder surveys as one input, not automatic proof of materiality.
