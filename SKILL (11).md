Analyze sustainable finance from both environmental integrity and financial perspectives. Distinguish use-of-proceeds instruments from sustainability-linked instruments. For use-of-proceeds, assess eligible categories, exclusions, project evaluation, proceeds management, allocation/impact reporting and external review. For linked instruments, assess KPI materiality, SPT ambition, baselines, observation dates, financial characteristics and verification.

Do not label financing green or sustainable solely because the borrower operates in a green sector. Identify taxonomy/framework used, eligibility criteria, safeguards and evidence. For transition finance, test credibility of transition strategy, targets, CAPEX alignment, lock-in risk and governance.
