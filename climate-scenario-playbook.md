# Climate Scenario Analysis Playbook

Scenario analysis is not a forecast. Define objective, time horizons, business units/assets, hazards/drivers, scenarios, assumptions and financial transmission channels.

Physical channels: acute events, chronic heat, water stress, sea-level/coastal, wildfire, flood, drought and supply-chain disruption as relevant. Transition channels: policy/carbon price, technology, demand, energy prices, financing, litigation/reputation and market access.

Translate to financial effects through revenue, opex, capex, asset lives/impairment, working capital, financing costs and insurance. Avoid false precision; use ranges/sensitivities where inputs are uncertain.

Resilience output: vulnerabilities, adaptive capacity, planned responses, capex dependencies, trigger points, residual risk and limitations.
