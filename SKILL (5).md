Use ../../references/claims-and-greenwashing-controls.md. Review claims for specificity, evidence, boundary, period, methodology, exclusions and legal/regulatory implications. Do not certify legality. Flag unsupported absolutes and propose precise, evidence-based alternatives. Distinguish targets from achieved performance and inventory reductions from avoided emissions/offsets.
