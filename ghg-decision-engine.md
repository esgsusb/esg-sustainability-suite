# GHG Accounting Decision Engine

## Boundary first
Determine reporting entity, consolidation approach, reporting period, base year, gases, GWP basis, facilities/assets/SPVs, and whether the question concerns corporate inventory, project accounting, financed emissions, or avoided emissions. Never mix these ledgers.

## Classification sequence
1. Is the source owned/controlled under the selected organizational boundary? Consider Scope 1.
2. Is it purchased/acquired electricity, steam, heat or cooling? Consider Scope 2.
3. Otherwise assess Scope 3 category based on value-chain relationship.
4. For investments/financing, assess Category 15 and the nature of the instrument.
5. For an SPV, separately determine the SPV's own Scope 1/2/3 inventory and the investor's attribution in Category 15.

## Category 15 / project finance
Record: instrument, investor role, outstanding amount, total project cost/equity/debt as required by chosen method, ownership/control, project emissions, reporting-year timing, and methodology. GHG Protocol project-finance guidance allocates relevant project Scope 1/2 using proportional share of total project costs (equity + debt); significant project Scope 3 may also be relevant. PCAF may be selected for financial-industry financed-emissions accounting; document asset class, attribution factor and data-quality score. Do not substitute equity share merely because an equity stake exists if the chosen project-finance method specifies a different denominator.

## Emission-factor hierarchy
Prefer, where methodologically appropriate: supplier/facility-specific verified factors -> contractual/residual mix for relevant Scope 2 treatment -> country/grid/fuel/process-specific authoritative factors -> recognized lifecycle databases -> EEIO/spend-based screening. Record source, geography, year, units, GWP basis, technology, uncertainty and conversion.

## Data quality
Score each material line item for source reliability, temporal match, geographic match, technological match, completeness and verification. Escalate estimates that are both material and low quality.

## Controls
Require unit checks, duplicate checks, boundary reconciliation, source-to-calculation trace, factor-version lock, formula review, year-on-year variance analysis and management sign-off.
