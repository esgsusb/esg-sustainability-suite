# Framework Registry and Source Hierarchy

Use primary standard-setter sources first. Do not reproduce proprietary standards verbatim; summarize requirements and direct users to official sources. Always record the edition/effective date actually applied.

## IFRS Sustainability / ISSB
- IFRS S1: investor-focused sustainability-related financial risks and opportunities; core content is governance, strategy, risk management, metrics and targets. Effective for annual periods beginning on/after 1 Jan 2024.
- IFRS S2: climate-related risks/opportunities, used with IFRS S1; includes physical/transition risk, scenario analysis, GHG emissions and industry-based information.
- Official navigator: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/
- Implementation materials: https://www.ifrs.org/supporting-implementation/supporting-materials-for-ifrs-sustainability-disclosure-standards/
- Licensing caution: integration/reproduction in products may require IFRS Foundation permission. Do not embed copyrighted standard text.

## GHG Protocol
- Corporate Standard: organizational/operational inventory foundation.
- Scope 2 Guidance: purchased electricity/steam/heat/cooling accounting.
- Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard + Calculation Guidance: 15 Scope 3 categories and method selection.
- Category 15 guidance: project finance/debt with known use of proceeds can use proportional share of total project costs (equity + debt), with project-specific data preferred where available.
- Official standards: https://ghgprotocol.org/standards-guidance

## PCAF
- Global GHG Accounting and Reporting Standard for the Financial Industry.
- Part A Financed Emissions, 3rd edition (2025) is the current Part A listed by PCAF as of Sep 2026.
- Covers asset-class methodologies including project finance.
- Official: https://carbonaccountingfinancials.com/standard

## ESRS / EFRAG
- For 2023 ESRS implementation, EFRAG IG1 Materiality Assessment, IG2 Value Chain, and IG3 Detailed Datapoints are key non-authoritative implementation guidance.
- In 2026, revised ESRS materials are evolving; verify the applicable legal/adopted version before asserting compliance. Do not mix draft/revised datapoints with the reporting entity's legally applicable ESRS set.
- Official implementation guidance: https://www.efrag.org/en/projects/esrs-implementation-guidance-documents

## GRI
- Modular system: Universal, Sector and Topic Standards.
- Universal Standards GRI 1/2/3 apply to all organizations using GRI.
- Verify current effective dates for revised Topic/Sector Standards; e.g. Biodiversity GRI 101 is effective from Jan 2026, while Climate Change/Energy GRI 102/103 (2025) become effective from Jan 2027.
- Official standards: https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/

## Source hierarchy
1. Applicable law/regulation and adopted delegated acts.
2. Official standard and authoritative amendments.
3. Official implementation guidance/FAQs/knowledge hubs.
4. Recognized sector methodologies.
5. Company policy/methodology.
6. Reputable secondary interpretation.

When sources conflict, do not silently harmonize. Identify the conflict, applicable reporting objective, hierarchy and decision owner.
