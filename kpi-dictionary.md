# ESG KPI Dictionary Template

Each KPI should have: KPI ID; name; purpose; framework mapping; precise definition; numerator; denominator; unit; organizational boundary; operational boundary; value-chain boundary; frequency; data owner; source system; calculation formula; emission factor/source where relevant; estimation rule; exclusions; restatement threshold; target link; control owner; evidence; assurance status.

## Core domains
Climate: Scope 1, Scope 2 location/market where applicable, Scope 3 by category, energy, renewable electricity, intensity, target progress, carbon price exposure.
Environment: water withdrawal/consumption/discharge, waste by route, biodiversity/location metrics where material.
Social: workforce, turnover, training, diversity metrics as legally appropriate, health & safety, contractor safety, human-rights due diligence.
Governance: board/management oversight, ethics/compliance, incidents, supplier due diligence, policy coverage.
Finance: climate-related capex/opex, current/anticipated financial effects, sustainable finance instruments, financed emissions where applicable.
