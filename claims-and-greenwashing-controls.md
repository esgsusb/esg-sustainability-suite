# ESG Claims and Greenwashing Controls

Before external use, classify claim: factual performance; target; comparative; product/project label; carbon-neutral/net-zero; renewable/green; avoided emissions; alignment/compliance claim.

Apply tests: specific scope; defined boundary; time period; methodology; baseline; evidence; material exclusions; uncertainty; attribution; target status; offsets/credits treatment; legal/regulatory review.

Red flags: absolute terms without scope, cherry-picked KPIs, mixing avoided emissions with inventory reductions, implying certification that does not exist, presenting estimates as measured data, unsupported 'Paris-aligned' or 'net zero' language, double counting environmental attributes, and claiming framework compliance without a complete requirement assessment.

Output should propose narrower evidence-based wording when a claim is not supportable.
