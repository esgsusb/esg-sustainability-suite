# Materiality Playbook

Do not collapse different materiality concepts.

ISSB/IFRS S1-S2: information material to primary users about sustainability-related risks/opportunities that could reasonably affect prospects.
GRI: material topics reflect the organization's most significant impacts on economy, environment and people, including human rights.
ESRS: double materiality combines impact materiality and financial materiality; a sustainability matter can be material from either or both perspectives.

Workflow: context -> long list -> evidence/stakeholders -> identify impacts/risks/opportunities -> assess using framework-specific criteria -> validate thresholds/judgments -> governance approval -> map disclosures/KPIs -> maintain change log.

Never average impact and financial scores into a single number unless the applicable methodology explicitly supports it; preserve each lens.
